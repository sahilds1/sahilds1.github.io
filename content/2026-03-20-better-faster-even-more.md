Title: TIL: WORKLOG.md for picking up AI coding sessions
Date: 2026-03-20
Category: Today I Learned
Status: published

I started using [Rands](https://randsinrepose.com/)'s practice of creating a WORKLOG.md to pick up where I left off with Claude.

Instruction for logging to WORKLOG.md:
>Logs what we investigated, what changed, what we decided, and why

Instruction for reading WORKLOG.md:
>Read the worklog and picks up where we left off instead of starting cold.

Instruction for compressing WORKLOG.md:
>Delete anything recoverable from git history or the current code, condense old completed work, but keep open questions, key decisions, and active experiments.
